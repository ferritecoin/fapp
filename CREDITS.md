# Credits

Ferritext is made with the work of many people, freely shared. Thank you.

The app shows this list too (Settings › About › Open source). Each project's
full licence comes with it; the fonts' licences are in `data/fonts/`.

| Project | What it does here | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| [Python](https://www.python.org/) | the language it is written in | PSF License 2.0 |
| [Kivy](https://kivy.org/) | its windows, touch and drawing | MIT |
| [cryptography](https://cryptography.io/) | the sealing: AES-GCM, HKDF, scrypt | Apache 2.0 or BSD |
| [Pillow](https://python-pillow.org/) | photos and pictures, made small | HPND |
| [requests](https://requests.readthedocs.io/), [urllib3](https://urllib3.readthedocs.io/), [idna](https://github.com/kjd/idna), [charset-normalizer](https://github.com/jawah/charset_normalizer) | talking to the relay | Apache 2.0, MIT, BSD, MIT |
